## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Anchor
The design system positions real estate content creation not as transactional marketing puffery, but as an empirical research discipline. The brand voice communicates rigorous technical literacy, clarity of scope, and unimpeachable operational transparency. The interface eliminates speculative hype—declining exaggerated flourishes in favor of audit-ready precision, verifiable data structures, and crisp analytical hierarchy. 

### Target Audience & Psychological Intent
- **Primary Audience:** Real estate developers, brokerage executives, commercial leasing managers, and institutional property marketers requiring accurate, compliant content solutions.
- **Evoked Feeling:** Institutional trust, operational clarity, scientific rigor, and administrative competence. The visual environment mimics high-grade intelligence dashboards and peer-reviewed technical publications.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Analytical Modernism / Technical Corporate**: Grounded in high-density typography, precision-ruled structural layouts, crisp semantic borders, and disciplined surface tiers. It avoids playful micro-interactions and superficial decorative gradients, relying instead on clean spatial rhythm, monospaced metadata chips, and distinct data tables.

## Colors

### Strategic Palette Assignment
- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Navy Slate. Embodies executive weight, architectural authority, and legal-grade documentation structure. Applied to dominant headers, terminal action buttons, structured tabular key lines, and high-emphasis focal items.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`)**: Signal Sky Blue. Represents empirical clarity, dynamic progress metrics, active navigation states, and operational service tokens (`SVC-01` through `SVC-06`).
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`)**: Laboratory Teal. Used deliberately for analytical callouts, research badges, verified scope checkpoints, and metadata tag groupings.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Gray. Drives body readability, peripheral metadata strings, inactive dividers, and table sub-grids without visual strain.

### Surface System
The baseline background operates strictly on an ultra-clean `#F8FAFC` foundation paired with elevated `#FFFFFF` surfaces. High-contrast typography ensures uncompromised legibility for Korean business typography across dense technical summaries and audit tables.

## Typography

### Typesetting Philosophy
Typography mirrors research-grade publishing standards. Inter is chosen for its geometric purity, balanced numerical metrics, and flawless optical alignment alongside standard Korean business font stacks (such as Pretendard and system-ui sans-serif fallbacks).

### Editorial Layout Constraints
Align text components with the defined content scale:
- **30-Character Micro-Tier (`headline-sm` / `body-lg bold`):** Dedicated to high-impact brand statements and single-line value propositions.
- **100-Character Abstract Tier (`body-md`):** Card-level descriptions and quick-scan summaries.
- **300-Character Executive Tier (`body-md` / `line-height: 22px`):** Standard service scopes and client qualification criteria.
- **600-Character Audit Tier (`body-lg` / `body-md` columns):** Full-length methodology disclosures, operational roadmaps, and delivery manifestos.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Model
A 12-column responsive fluid grid operates across all standard viewport envelopes, bound by a maximum width of `1280px` for desktop presentations. 

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` (20px) outer canvas margins.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Vertical cadence adheres strictly to the 4px baseline grid (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Dense tabular layouts leverage `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact information display, while strategic section margins utilize `space-xl` and canvas margins to prevent visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

### Surface Architecture
Visual hierarchy is articulated through tonal layering and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy skeuomorphic shadows. The depth model emphasizes structural clarity:

1. **Canvas Level (0dp):** Tone `#F8FAFC`. Base canvas layer hosting structured section wrappers.
2. **Container Level (Flat Outline):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a razor-thin 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Flat elevation.
3. **Elevated Card / Modal (Micro-Elevation):** Low-contrast border (`#CBD5E1`) combined with an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
4. **Active Selection State:** Accent edge highlight using `border-color: #0284C7` with an inner tonal ring rather than vertical displacement.

## Shapes

### Edge Treatment
The design system enforces a **Soft (`1`)** shape language. Sharp technical clarity is preserved by avoiding bulbous corners while softening harsh brutalist points:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `rounded` = `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-lg` = `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Dialogs & High-Level Modals:** `rounded-xl` = `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Metric Badges & Status Chips:** `rounded` (4px) or flat rectangular tags with 1px inset borders to maintain a laboratory index aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0F172A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Transitions on hover to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, `#0F172A` text. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with `#0F172A` border.
- **Technical/Accent Action:** `#0284C7` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for conversion steps and document downloads.

### Chips & Taxonomy Identifiers (e.g., `SVC-01`, `SVC-02`)
- **Structure:** Monospaced styling using `label-code`. Height `24px`, horizontal padding `8px`, radius `4px`.
- **Styling:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`. 
- **Verified Labs Chips:** Background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.25)`, text `#0D9488`.

### Tables & Data Comparison Sheets
- **Header:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-caps` typography, text `#64748B`, bottom border 2px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Data Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` divider, padding `12px 16px`. Hover state applies `#F8FAFC`.
- **Alignment:** Aligns numeric delivery quantities (e.g., "3장", "1건", "1부") to the right; aligns taxonomy IDs and scope descriptions to the left.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Wrapper:** Height `42px`, padding `0 12px`, radius `4px`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Focus creates an immediate crisp ring: `1px solid #0284C7`.
- **Labels:** Set in `body-sm` with medium weight (`500`), sitting `6px` directly above the input surface.

### Cards & Service Containers
- Multi-tier cards use crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces wrapped in 1px `#E2E8F0`. 
- Card headers feature an explicit taxonomic code flag (`SVC-XX`) paired with character-count-governed title hierarchy. Content sections are segmented using horizontal `1px solid #F1F5F9` structural dividers.
